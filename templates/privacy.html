Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

Spendly is a personal expense tracking app. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the Service, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Data We Collect

We collect only the information needed to operate the Service:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Usage data — basic technical information such as browser type and pages visited, used to keep the Service running reliably.

We do not collect bank credentials, card numbers, or access to any of your financial accounts. All entries in Spendly are entered manually by you.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to provide and improve the Service:

  • To create and maintain your account and authenticate you.
  • To store and display your expense records, summaries, and reports.
  • To send service-related communications, such as account notices.
  • To diagnose issues and maintain the security of the Service.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or financial data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secured servers and transmitted over encrypted connections. Passwords are stored as irreversible hashes, never in plain text. While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You may request an export of your data, or the deletion of your account and all associated expense records, at any time by contacting us. Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

5. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on a small number of third-party providers to operate, such as hosting and email delivery services. These providers process data only on our behalf and are bound by confidentiality obligations. We may also embed web fonts or similar static resources, which may be requested directly from the provider's servers.

We do not include advertising trackers or analytics that profile your financial activity. If this ever changes, this policy will be updated before those changes take effect.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be announced on the Service before they take effect.

7. Contact Us

Questions, requests, or concerns about your privacy can be directed to privacy@spendly.example.
